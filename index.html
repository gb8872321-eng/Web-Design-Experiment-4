Healthy Living Tips for Students

Small habits that make a big difference

Food Exercise Sleep Subscribe

Eat Healthy Food

Try to eat fruits, vegetables and home-made food every day. Drink enough water and avoid too much junk food.

Do Regular Exercise

Walking or doing yoga for 30 minutes daily keeps the body active and the mind fresh.

Get Enough Sleep

Students should sleep for 7 to 8 hours. Good sleep helps in studying and remembering things.

Quick Tip

Keep your phone away one hour before sleeping.
